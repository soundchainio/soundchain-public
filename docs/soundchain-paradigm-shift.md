### How It Works

1. **Artist uploads a track** - FREE via SCid (SoundChain ID certificate)
2. **SCid registers the track** on-chain with a unique identifier
3. **Every 30+ seconds of play** triggers a stream reward event
4. **70% goes to the creator** - the artist who uploaded
5. **30% goes to the listener** - the person who discovered and played it
6. **Both can claim OGUN** from their PiggyBank anytime

## Revenue Model: SoundChain's Income

SoundChain charges **0.05% on all on-chain transactions:**

| Transaction | Fee | Example |
|-------------|-----|---------|
| OGUN Claims (PiggyBank) | 0.05% of claimed amount | Claim 1000 OGUN → 0.5 OGUN fee |
| OGUN → USDC Swaps | 0.05% of swap amount | Swap 500 OGUN → 0.25 OGUN fee |
| USDC → OGUN Swaps | 0.05% of swap amount | Same |
| NFT Marketplace Sales | 0.05% of sale price | Sell for 100 POL → 0.05 POL fee |
| Token Transfers | 0.05% of amount | Send 200 OGUN → 0.1 OGUN fee |
| Staking/Unstaking | 0.05% of amount | Stake 1000 OGUN → 0.5 OGUN fee |

**As the platform grows, transaction volume grows, fees compound.**
