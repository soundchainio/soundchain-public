🌟 SoundChain — How to Use
A plain-language guide to the SoundChain desktop apps · v0.1 beta

1. What you've got

SoundChain ships as real downloadable apps — files you own, not a website you visit. There are two kinds:

🛰️ North Star (the sovereign node)

A peer-to-peer app with no server behind it. When you and friends open it on the same Wi-Fi, your apps find each other and share posts directly — no cloud, no account, no company in the middle. It even works with the internet unplugged.

🎵 The SoundChain apps (SoundChain · Lucy · Arena · Mint)

Desktop versions of the live SoundChain experience — the music platform, the Lucy AI, the Arena sports hub, and the Mint marketplace — in their own windows, with their own app icons.

2. Installing & opening

macOS (.dmg)

  1. Double-click the .dmg, drag SoundChain into Applications.
  2. First launch: right-click the app → Open → Open. (The beta isn't notarized yet, so macOS asks once. After that it opens normally.)

Windows (.exe)

  1. Run the installer (or the portable .exe — no install needed).
  2. If SmartScreen warns: More info → Run anyway (same beta-signing reason).

Linux (.AppImage)

  1. Make it runnable: chmod +x SoundChain*.AppImage
  2. Double-click it, or run it from the terminal.
Heads-up: the unsigned/beta warning on every OS is normal for a direct-download app and goes away once we add code-signing certificates. It does not mean the app is unsafe.

3. Using North Star (the p2p node)

  1. Open the app on two or more machines on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Watch the pill near the top: ● solo turns into ● 2 peers · no server when it finds the others — that's your private mesh forming.
  3. Type in the box at the bottom and hit Post (or Enter).
  4. Your post appears on every machine, instantly — with nothing in between.
  5. Close and reopen — your posts are still there (saved locally on each device).
Try this: turn off Wi-Fi's internet (or pull the router's WAN cable) but stay on the same local network. Posts still sync. That's the whole point — the network is the people, not a data center.

4. Navigating the SoundChain apps

  • SoundChain — your feed, profile/wall, music, and circle. Posts autoplay inline as you scroll.
  • Lucy — your AI. Type to chat; she runs toward on-device intelligence.
  • Arena — live sports takes, stats, picks and fantasy.
  • Mint — the NFT/marketplace surface for creating and collecting.

Each app is its own window with its own icon, so you can keep them side-by-side like any other desktop apps.

5. Pricing & payment

Long-time L1 + L2 members get the full app bundle free, as a thank-you. For everyone else it's a one-time $15 — a file you own, forever.

Pay your way

Pay in USDC (the anchor — exactly $15, no volatility) · BTC · ETH · SOL · or $OGUN (the native token, discounted). Prefer a card? A fiat on-ramp lets you pay in your local currency (USD, GBP, JPY…) and it settles automatically.

6. Your Pass & activation

Your purchase — or your L1/L2 status — is a North Star Pass: a token that lives in your wallet. It's the key, and it can't be revoked or switched off by anyone.

  1. Open the app. If activation is enabled, you'll see the Activate screen.
  2. Step 1: copy the message shown and sign it in your wallet — free, and it sends no transaction.
  3. Step 2: paste the signature back. The app reads the chain directly to confirm your Pass — no server, no account.
  4. Verified once, the device stays unlocked.
Why this way: the license is an asset you own, not a row in someone's database — the same spirit as the app itself: free, open, and yours.
