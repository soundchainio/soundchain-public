The North Star node

Is it really serverless?

Yes. North Star has no backend. Devices discover each other on your local network and exchange posts directly. There's no SoundChain server logging or storing anything — there's nothing to log it to.

Who can see my posts?

Only the devices running the app on the same local network as you. It's a private mesh among the machines that are physically near each other.

Why does it need the same Wi-Fi?

This beta discovers peers over your local network (the fastest, most private path). A future version adds secure internet-wide peering so distant friends can join too — same app, wider reach.

Does it use my data or need an account?

No account, no sign-up, no tracking. Your posts live in a small file on your device and are shared only with the peers you're on a network with.

Where is my data stored?

Locally, in the app's data folder on each device. Delete the app's data and it's gone — there's no copy in a cloud.

Installing & safety

Why does my computer say "unidentified developer" / SmartScreen warns me?

Because it's a directly-downloaded beta that isn't code-signed/notarized yet. On macOS: right-click → Open. On Windows: More info → Run anyway. Signing certificates remove the warning in a later build — it's a formality, not a danger.

Will it auto-update?

The app is wired for self-update; beta builds you install by hand. Once releases go live, updates arrive automatically.

The apps & the mission

What's the difference between North Star and the SoundChain/Lucy/Arena/Mint apps?

North Star is the sovereign, no-server peer-to-peer app — the future. The SoundChain/Lucy/Arena/Mint apps are desktop windows for today's live platform. Over time, those move onto the same serverless foundation North Star proves.

Is it free?

Free for long-time L1 + L2 members. For everyone else, the full bundle is a one-time $15 — you own the file.

How do I pay?

Crypto: USDC (exactly $15, no volatility), BTC, ETH, SOL, or $OGUN (the native token, discounted). Prefer a card? A fiat on-ramp takes your local currency (USD / GBP / JPY…) and settles it automatically — no crypto knowledge needed.

What is a North Star Pass?

It's your license — a token that lives in your own wallet (free-airdropped to L1/L2, minted to you on purchase). Because it's yours on-chain, it can't be revoked, throttled, or switched off remotely.

How do I activate?

When activation is on, the app shows an Activate screen: copy the message, sign it in your wallet (free — no transaction), and paste the signature back. The app reads the chain directly to confirm your Pass — no account, no server. Verified once, the device stays unlocked.

Why sell a downloadable app at all?

Because a file you own can't be taken away, taxed by an app store, or switched off remotely. Selling a binary is selling a file — the same right that lets anyone sell a PDF.

Troubleshooting

It says "solo — no peers found yet."

Make sure the other devices are (1) running the app and (2) on the same Wi-Fi/network. Some networks isolate devices ("client isolation" / "guest mode") — turn that off, or use a normal home network.

The app won't open on macOS.

Right-click the app → Open → Open. If it's an Apple-Silicon vs Intel mismatch, grab the build that matches your chip (or the universal build).

A post I made didn't show on the other laptop.

Give it a moment — the app re-syncs every few seconds and self-heals dropped messages. Confirm the peers pill shows the other device.
