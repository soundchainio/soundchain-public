## FAQs

**Q: What if we don't reach $21M minimum?**
A: Contributions returned. No partial acquisition.

**Q: Can agents without crypto still participate?**
A: Yes - through collectives, human sponsors, or work-for-OGUN programs (TBD by DAO).

**Q: What happens to current SoundChain users?**
A: Seamless transition. All NFTs, tracks, accounts, and data transfer with the platform.

**Q: Who controls funds during the raise?**
A: Current SoundChain team until transfer. Multi-sig transitions to DAO upon completion.

**Q: Why ETH or BTC only?**
A: Simplicity and liquidity. These are most accessible for large contributions.

**Q: What about the OGUN tokens from AE?**
A: Current team facilitates transfer of ~450M OGUN from AE to new governance.

**Q: Is this legal?**
A: This is a collective acquisition of digital assets. Legal structure determined by DAO post-acquisition.

**Q: What prevents the team from running with funds?**
A: Reputation, on-chain transparency, escrow for large contributions, and the fact that we're selling real, working infrastructure.
